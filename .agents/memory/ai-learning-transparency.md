---
name: AI learning transparency
description: Learning dashboard must expose sample quality, outcomes, market ranking, and ROI limitations
---

AI learning must never be represented by hit rate alone. The dashboard should show the settled sample, W/L/partial/PUSH breakdown, excluded NO BET count, market-level ranking, and the data-quality threshold behind any recommendation.

**Why:** A 50% hit rate can hide a small sample, negative ROI, partial outcomes, or a market-specific signal; users need to distinguish observed evidence from an automatic decision.

**How to apply:** Keep recommendations fail-closed until a market has at least three samples, label fewer than ten settled outcomes as an early signal, and calculate ROI only from valid settled odds.
